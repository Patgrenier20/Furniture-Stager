### Full description

Turn workshop photos into polished furniture listings with FurniFlip.

FurniFlip is built for furniture flippers, refinishers, and resale sellers who want a faster path from a finished piece to a professional-looking listing. Upload a photo, clean up distracting backgrounds, place the piece in a styled room, and create listing copy from one focused workspace.

Use FurniFlip to:

- Remove cluttered or distracting photo backgrounds.
- Create clean furniture cutouts for reuse in listings.
- Stage furniture in realistic lifestyle settings.
- Generate marketplace-ready titles and descriptions.
- Explore a guided refinish-to-list workflow.
- Keep projects and generated images organized in one account.

Basic access is available without a credit card. AI-generated results can vary, so review images and listing details before publishing them.

FurniFlip does not list or sell furniture on your behalf and is not affiliated with Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist, or other resale marketplaces.
